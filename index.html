I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I am supervised by Prof. Lihua Xie and co-supervised by Prof. Jianfei Yang.

Overview

My research focuses on human-centered AI, enabling AI agents, robots, IoT systems, and healthcare devices to perceive, understand, and interact with humans. My long-term vision is to build warm and trustworthy AI assistants with strong human awareness, supporting daily living and healthcare.

My current work advances this goal along three axes: privacy-preserving motion capture using low-cost RF sensors such as mmWave radar; human motion modeling with advanced generative AI; and healthcare AI for monitoring physical and mental well-being with IoT devices.
