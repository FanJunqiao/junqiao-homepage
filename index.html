I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I am supervised by Prof. Lihua Xie and co-supervised by Prof. Jianfei Yang.

My research focuses on human sensing, embodied AI, and generative AI. I am particularly interested in privacy-preserving human motion capture with RF sensors, mmWave radar perception, human pose and mesh estimation, and human-centered physical AI systems for healthcare and daily assistance.

  • Human Sensing: human motion capture, motion generation, pose and mesh estimation.
  • Embodied AI: multimodal perception, robot learning, human-robot interaction.
  • Generative AI: diffusion and flow models for human motion and data generation.
